# FixifAI — Knowledge Base (for WhatsApp AI Agent)

## What is FixifAI?

FixifAI is an AI-powered maintenance management platform built by CloudIT
Solutions (Pvt) Ltd. It helps maintenance and field-service businesses run their
entire operation from one screen: job tracking, technician management, customer
communication, quotations, invoices, and maintenance contracts.

Tagline: "Run your maintenance business from one screen."

Website & pilot signup: https://fixifai.cloudit.lk/#signup

## Who is it for?

- Service contractors: AC & refrigeration service companies, lift/elevator
  maintenance, generator service, fire & security, CCTV installers, facilities
  management companies.
- In-house maintenance teams: factories, hotels, hospitals, apartment complexes
  and office buildings that maintain their own assets.

## Key features

- QR code on every asset: customers scan to report a problem instantly — the
  system already knows the asset, its history, warranty and contract.
- AI job intake: the customer describes the problem (text or photo) and AI
  creates a categorized, prioritized job with the right follow-up questions.
- GPS-verified technicians: technicians check in on-site with GPS, so owners
  can prove visits happened. Before/after photos and customer signature included.
- Voice-to-service-report: technician speaks, AI writes the structured report.
- Quotations & invoices: create on-site, customer approves with one tap,
  online payment via PayHere.
- AMC / contract automation: recurring preventive-maintenance jobs created
  automatically, renewal reminders so no contract slips.
- Customer portal: each client gets a branded portal with their assets,
  service history, job tracking and approvals.
- Manager dashboard: kanban job board, weekly schedule, live technician map,
  reports (revenue, jobs, repeat faults).
- Works in English; Sinhala and Tamil support is planned.
- WhatsApp integration is an optional add-on (automation package), not required.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: Is it really free for 3 months?
A: Yes. The pilot is genuinely free for 3 months, no card required. You get the
full product while we learn from your feedback.

Q: Do technicians need training?
A: No. The technician app is simpler than WhatsApp — if your team can use a
smartphone, they can use FixifAI from day one.

Q: What happens after the pilot?
A: You decide. Founding customers get 50% off Year 1 if they continue — no
lock-in, no cancellation hassle.

Q: Do my customers need to install an app?
A: No. Customers simply scan the QR tag on the equipment or open your service
link in any browser — nothing to install.

Q: Does it work in Sinhala/Tamil?
A: Not yet — the pilot is English-first. Sinhala and Tamil support is planned
for a later phase.
